Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Welcome to Dough & Crest. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, ordering food, contacting us, subscribing to our newsletter, or using any of our online services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you use our website, including:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery or billing address, where applicable
  • Information submitted through our contact forms
  • Order and booking information
  • Newsletter subscription information
  • Any other information you choose to provide to us

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically. This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, referring website, date and time of your visit, and general website usage information.

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website and improve its performance, security, and functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

Dough & Crest may use the information collected for purposes such as:

  • Processing and managing food orders
  • Responding to enquiries and contact requests
  • Providing customer support
  • Managing reservations or catering enquiries
  • Sending requested information
  • Sending newsletters or promotional communications when you have subscribed
  • Improving our website, products, and services
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Maintaining website security
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

4. Newsletter Subscriptions

If you subscribe to the Dough & Crest newsletter, we may use your email address to send you restaurant news, promotions, special offers, menu updates, and other relevant communications.

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in the communication or by contacting us directly.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember preferences, understand website traffic, and improve website functionality.

You can configure your browser to reject or delete cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some features of the website.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to support website hosting, analytics, payment processing, email delivery, online ordering, reservations, advertising, or other business functions.

These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle information in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements.

7. Payment Information

Where online payments are available, payment transactions may be processed by third-party payment providers. Dough & Crest generally does not directly store complete payment card details on its own website servers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information for money. We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide requested services, process orders or payments, communicate with you, protect our website, or comply with legal requirements.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including fulfilling orders, providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and meeting legal obligations.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or other appropriate handling of your information.

You may also opt out of promotional communications at any time.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

13. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, payment services, delivery services, or other external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
